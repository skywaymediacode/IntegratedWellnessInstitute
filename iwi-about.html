About The Institute

About Course Development

At the Integrated Wellness Institute, we take immense pride in the quality and expertise behind every course we offer. Each course is meticulously crafted by a doctorate-level practitioner with a wealth of experience in therapy and counselor education.

Our courses are not just educational; they are designed with precise objectives aimed at empowering you to achieve your wellness goals. The Integrated Wellness Institute offers expert-developed courses that help students dive deeper into various areas of wellness.

IWI is an NBCC Approved Continuing Education Provider, ACEP No. 7535. Continuing education credit applies to Helping Professionals provider courses.

About The Founder

Dr. Sarah Stewart-Spencer
  • Licensed Professional Counselor, Virginia
  • Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Florida
  • Nationally Certified Counselor

Dr. Sarah Stewart-Spencer founded the Integrated Wellness Institute on the conviction that everyone can develop the insight, skills, and strategies needed to live better. She holds certifications as a First Responder Provider, Hypnotherapist and Clinical Trauma Professional.

Roles
Owner of the Integrated Wellness Institute and Stewart-Spencer Consulting. Associate Professor at The Chicago School of Professional Psychology and the University of West Alabama. Course developer at the International Trauma Training Institute, and a private practice provider.
Previously
Dean of Behavioural Sciences at Yorkville University.
Editorial
Editor and founder of Therapeutic Speakeasy Quarterly. Former editor of The Humanistic Counselor.
Credentials
LMHC, LPC, NCC, CH, CCTP

Course Developers

Dr. Allison Kanter Agliata

Ph.D., BCC, CFRP

Licensed clinical psychologist and board-certified executive coach, and a former Air Force Captain. She holds an Advanced Educational Leadership certification from Harvard University and founded Dr. Allison & Associates.

Former Training Psychologist for U.S. Special Operations Command, author of Mastering Resiliency: First Responders Edition, and a founding board member of the Suncoast Alliance of First Responders.

Dr. Sofia Georgiadou

Ph.D., LMFT-S, LPC-S, NCC

Licensed marriage and family therapist supervisor and licensed professional counselor supervisor, with over 16 years of clinical experience working with trauma-impacted populations. Formerly Clinical Director at a residential treatment center.

An experienced educator and curriculum developer, she hosts the Learning and Teaching Systemic Therapy podcast and co-edits Trauma-Informed Relational Therapy: Applications and Interventions, forthcoming from Springer Publishing in 2026.
